Find your way around Magpie

The top-bar workspace selector determines which proxies, sources, settings, rotators, and alerts you see. Check it first when a list looks empty or another member sees different data. Your account and invitation inbox span workspaces.

Open the bell in the top right to see your five most recent notifications, newest first. It combines pending workspace invitations, incidents in the selected workspace, and Magpie releases. The order uses the invitation's creation time, the incident's opening time, or the release's publication time.

The badge counts unread items among those five notifications. Unread rows have a dot and stronger text. Selecting a notification marks only that event as read; opening the menu or using its footer links does not mark events as read. Read items stay in their original time order.

Read status belongs to your account and is saved on the server, so it survives sign-out, restarts, and use in another browser. The menu checks it when opened or refreshed and when you return to the browser. Reading an invitation does not accept it, and reading an alert does not close the incident.

The releases page uses the same account read status. Opening release notes marks that release as read, and Mark all as read marks the releases shown in the archive without marking invitations or alerts.

Select an item to open the invitation inbox, alert history, or that release's notes. Invitation and alert notifications scroll to the matching invitation or incident and highlight it for three seconds. Workspace invitations stays available at the bottom of the menu, even when invitations are outside the latest five. The menu refreshes when you open it; use its refresh button to check again while it is open.

The usual daily workflow​

  1. Select the workspace you intend to operate.
  2. Check its dashboard for a trend worth investigating.
  3. Open affected proxies or sources and inspect their latest evidence.
  4. Change the relevant workspace setting, then wait for new applicable checks.
  5. Confirm the usable pool on the rotator your application actually uses.

The diagnostic workflow helps you find which stage needs attention. Refreshing the dashboard repeatedly does not force a new proxy check.

Pages for instance administrators​

PageChanges
/global/checkerInstance worker limits, cycle intervals, and defaults.
/global/scraperInstance scraping limits, interval, and robots policy.
/global/blacklistIP blacklist sources and blocked website policy.
/pluginsEnrichment integrations.
/plugins/geoliteGeoLite configuration.
/plugins/abuseipdbAbuseIPDB configuration.
/plugins/ipgeolocationIPGeolocation.io credentials, cache policy, and credit budget.

These pages require the global account admin role. A workspace owner does not automatically become an instance administrator. Conversely, an instance administrator still needs workspace membership to read its resources. See admin settings.

If a control is missing or disabled, inspect your workspace role and the resource's lifecycle before treating it as a loading failure. Viewers can read resources; operators and higher can operate them.
